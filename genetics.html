Genetic Diversity

An interactive map of the ancestry of U.S. honey bee populations, built from mitochondrial haplotype and lineage data in published studies and public sequence databases.

What you'll be able to do

  • See lineage makeup by regionMap the share of colonies from each major evolutionary lineage: C (eastern European), M (western and northern European), A (African), and O (Middle Eastern).
  • Explore haplotypesView how often specific mitochondrial haplotypes appear in each state or county.
  • Compare diversityShade the map by a diversity index to see where populations are more or less varied.
  • Track change over timeCompare survey years to follow shifts in ancestry and diversity.
  • Download what you seeExport the summarized frequencies behind any view as a CSV file.

Until the map launches

Read how lineages and diversity measures will be defined, or get in touch if you'd like to be notified when the map goes live.
