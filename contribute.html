Contribute

Share your data, keep the credit.

A national picture of honey bee health depends on data collected by many people. The Hive Atlas does the work of standardizing, mapping, and citing it, so your data reaches more people with your name attached.

What contributors get

The benefits depend on who you are and why you collect data.

Researchers

Get more use and citations from data you have already published.

  • A citable record for each dataset
  • Credit on every map view that uses it
  • A home that meets funder data-sharing requirements

State apiarists & inspectors

See your state's results in a national context without extra analysis work.

  • Free standardization of your records
  • Your state compared with national trends
  • Summaries you can share with stakeholders

Beekeepers & organizations

Help build the picture while keeping your operation private.

  • No apiary locations or names are ever shown
  • Results are combined at the county level or above
  • Optional embargo periods before public release

How contributing works

From first contact to a published dataset.

  1. Get in touch

    Tell us what data you have, its years, and its geographic coverage.

  2. Agree on terms

    We set credit, privacy, embargo, and license terms together in a short agreement.

  3. Share the files

    Send the data in its current format. Spreadsheets and CSV files are fine.

  4. Standardize & review

    We map your fields to The Hive Atlas format and send the summary back for your approval.

  5. Publish

    The dataset goes live with its citation, and appears on every map it informs.

Helpful fields to include

Data doesn't need to be in a special format. These fields make it easiest to use:

  • Collection date (or at least the year)
  • State and county of the apiary
  • What was tested and the method used
  • The result, as a count, load, or positive or negative
  • For genetic data, the haplotype or lineage call and method
